## What you may do without asking

- Build pucks for yourself, your crew, your camp — and call them Firefly pucks. That's the point.
- Fork, modify, and redistribute the code (per the GPL), stating truthfully that your work is "based on Firefly."
- Write, post, or make videos about Firefly, including criticism, using the name and marks to refer to the project.
- Sell devices or kits built from this code **under your own name and branding** — the GPL guarantees this and we don't discourage it. Compatible pucks make every mesh better.

## What requires written permission

- Selling devices, kits, or services **under the "Firefly" name** or bearing the flare mark / wordmark.
- Using the name or marks in a way that implies your product or service is made, endorsed, or supported by this project.
- Registering domains, social accounts, or store listings whose branding is likely to be confused with the official project.

Ask via an issue on this repo or the contact in the README — community makers acting in good faith will generally hear "yes."

## Why this exists

This project's history includes a commercial predecessor that was withdrawn from its users. The GPL ensures the *code* can never be taken away; this trademark policy ensures the *name* keeps meaning the genuine, open article. It is the same model used by Arduino, Prusa, and Meshtastic® (whose trademark policy inspired this one).
